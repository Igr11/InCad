Constraint List

Every constraint of the sketch in one list, also the ones with no icon to click.

Pick a line or point to see only its own. Check rows and press Delete selected.

Or right-click a line or point and delete one of its constraints.

Projected edges are normal lines by default; Construction switches them.

Список связей показывает все связи эскиза, даже те, на которые нельзя кликнуть. Удаление одним шагом Undo.
