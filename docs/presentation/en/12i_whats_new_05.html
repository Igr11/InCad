Text and Emboss

Text in a sketch: two clicks draw its box — left / centre / right, top / middle / bottom, or Fit to the box.

Or along any curve: round a circle, flipped inside a ring like a coin. Any font, Bold, Italic, Mirror.

Emboss raises or engraves it — wrapped round cylinders and cones, or straight on a flat face.

Evenly round spreads the letters once round the whole face; Engrave can cut through like a stencil.

Version 0.5.0: Text (in a box or along a curve) and Emboss (wrapped round cylinders and cones).
