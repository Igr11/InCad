Split and Make Assembly

Split

The lines of a sketch cut the part. Keep all: each piece is a Solid. Keep picked: only the pieces you click stay.

Make Assembly

Each separate piece becomes a part file with its history; a new assembly links them where they were.

Run it again and choose Replace existing files: the old files are overwritten.

Split режет деталь линиями эскиза. Make Assembly превращает каждый отдельный кусок в файл детали с историей и собирает сборку.
